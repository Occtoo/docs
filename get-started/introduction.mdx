## One engine. All your product data. Every destination.

A Product Orchestration Engine is the layer between your enterprise systems and the world your customers experience. It takes product data from wherever it lives, structures it into a single trusted source, and distributes it to every channel, application, and AI agent that needs it — fast, clean and without custom integrations.

Most organizations store data in systems built for operations — PIM, WMS, OMS, CRM. That data is structured for the system, not for the customer. Getting it out, combining it, and serving it fast to a frontend takes weeks of integration work and still produces something rigid and slow. This is Enterprise state.

Experience state is different. Data is stripped from its original structure, normalized into a simple format, and made available as purpose-built endpoints — containing only what a specific application needs. Fast to build, fast to serve, easy to change.

Occtoo moves product data from Enterprise state to Experience state. Your team goes from waiting on integrations to shipping experiences.
