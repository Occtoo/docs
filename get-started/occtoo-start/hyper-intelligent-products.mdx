## Partner perspective

This phase is where partners move from **technical delivery** to **strategic value creation**.

Here, customers define **Hyper-intelligent Products**—products enriched with the data, context, and relationships needed to perform everywhere.

<Note>
This is often where partners shift from implementer to trusted advisor.
</Note>

---

## Defining the Master Product

A **Hyper-intelligent Product** is not a copy of ERP or commerce data.

It is a **Master Product** combining:

- Core product data
- Enrichment and contextual data
- Experience-specific attributes
- Relationships across variants, media, and markets

Partners help customers ask:

<Tip>
- Do we have intelligent product data today?
- What should our Master Product represent?
- Which data points accelerate checkout, pricing, and conversion?
</Tip>

---

## How partners model this in Occtoo

Partners collaborate with customers to:

- Map the intelligent product data landscape
- Define attributes and relationships
- Configure **Cards** and **Segments**
- Establish models designed to evolve

Occtoo’s flexible modeling supports both:

- Activation data (Destinations)
- Internal and process-support data

---

## Common partner pitfalls

<Warning>
- Replicating commerce or PIM models inside Occtoo
- Designing for today’s constraints instead of tomorrow’s needs
- Over-engineering before value is proven
</Warning>
