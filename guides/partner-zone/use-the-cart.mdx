The cart

- Add products & media to the cart
- Download all product data together with images
- Select image resolution

![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/cart-dacb112bb75def9ef3a623d38ac36759.JPG)

- When you are satisfied with your cart you can easily just create a package and download all the content

## How to share cart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press the share cart button">

    If you want to share the cart with other Partnerzone users you can press the **share cart** button

    ![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/share-cart-c31749166512c89055863d2b35c74131.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the cart and choose users">

    A window will then pop up where you will be able to choose what you want to name the cart, write a message and choose which users you want to share it with

    ![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/share-cart-window-a7ebc0c5ba9f5f1ffef30eb64dcb4f39.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>

All the users you chose to give access will be able to see your cart at their respective Partnerzones

![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/shared-cart-2a25d2f302f795e47411f8ad7078ba89.png)
