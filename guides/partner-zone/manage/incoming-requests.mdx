---
title: "Handle incoming user requests"
---

New user requests are placed in a pending state until handled. In the top right corner, you have the option to ‘Review and approve’ or ‘Reject’ a pending request.

![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/manage/partners-incoming-req2-7caa108af85fc81846d4b861fdde549a.png)

The ‘Review and approve’ option asks for some user-specific information in a pop-up window before the user can be registered to a Partner:

- Partner - Choose the Partner the user belongs to.
- Assortment - Choose the right level of assortment which shall be visible to that user.
- Default language - Choose the default language used by the user.

The red cross is the 'Reject' button. Click this in order to remove or reject an incoming request.
