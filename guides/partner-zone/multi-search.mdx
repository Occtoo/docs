---
title: "Use the multi search function"
---

You can use the multi-search function to find many products in a single search. Simply paste a list of products that you are looking for.

![An image from the static](/images/guides/partner-zone/multi-search-ba2bf71d16ef7e471daa3479b4c53cd4.JPG)

<Info>
The search and multi-search functions are customizable. Read more on the "How to customize Partner Zone" page.
</Info>
