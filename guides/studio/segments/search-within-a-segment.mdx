title: "Search within a segment"
---

When a segment is created you have the possibilty to search inside the segment

Open your segment by clicking on the segment.

Use the search bar at the top

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/segments/SearchInASegment-7a5d3fdb327652d756936471e9ce5e29.png)

<Info>
You are able to search on the attribute selected as "Card name"
</Info>
