---
title: "Search within a segment"
description: "Find a single entry among the entries of a segment."
---

Open a segment and go to the **Segmented entries** widget. The header shows how many entries the segment holds.

![Segmented entries widget](/images/guides/studio/segments/segment-detail.png)

- **Search by entry title** — look for one entry by its title.
- **Sort by** — change the order of the entries, for example **Entry title (A-Z)**.
- **Included entries** and **Excluded entries** — switch between the entries in the segment and the ones you excluded from the result.
- The expand icon opens the entries in full screen, which is useful for large segments.

![Segmented entries in full screen](/images/guides/studio/segments/segment-entries-expanded-focus.png)

<Info>
The search matches the property the card uses as its entry title — the one set with **Select as card name**. That property needs **full-text search** enabled among its source property capabilities for partial matches to work.
</Info>

Select an entry to open it. The entry has four tabs: **Properties**, **Change log**, **Relations** and **Used by**.
