---

The **Segments** page lists every segment in the tenant with its category, status, entry count and type.

![Segments list page](/images/guides/studio/segments/segments-list.png)

- **Search for segment name** — find a segment by name.
- **Sort by** — last updated, name, or date created, ascending or descending.
- **Filter** — narrow the list down by several properties at once.
- The star marks the segment as a favourite, which pins it to your dashboard.

## Filters

Press **Filter** to open the panel. The footer shows how many segments match before you apply anything.

![Filters panel on the segments list](/images/guides/studio/segments/segments-list-filters.png)

| Filter | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Created by | The users who created segments |
| Category | The segment categories in the tenant |
| Status | Active, Archived, Scheduled, Initializing |
| Active Period | Starts before, Ends before, In between (range) |
| Usage | In use, Not in use |

![Status filter values](/images/guides/studio/segments/segments-list-filter-status.png)

Press **Apply** to filter the list, or **Clear all filters** to reset. The **Filter** button shows how many filters are active.

<Info>
**Usage** is the quickest way to find segments that no destination consumes, and **Active Period** to find segments that are about to end. See [Schedule a segment](/guides/studio/segments/schedule-a-segment) for what each status means.
</Info>

## Badges on a segment

- **Category** — the group the segment belongs to, above the name.
- **Status** — Active, Scheduled, Initializing or Archived.
- **Entries** — how many entries the segment currently holds.
- **Query** or **Fixed** — whether entries update automatically with the data, or stay as selected.
