Your avatar sits at the far right of the top bar, showing your initials. Hovering it reveals your name and email; clicking it opens the menu.

![The user menu](/images/guides/studio/dashboard/dashboard-user-menu.png)

## Edit your profile

Select **Profile** to open **Edit your profile**.

![The Edit your profile dialog](/images/guides/studio/dashboard/dashboard-profile-dialog.png)

**Given name** and **Family name** are editable. **Email** is shown but cannot be changed here — it is the identity you sign in with.

Your name is what appears wherever Studio attributes a change: the _Last updated by_ line on a card, source or segment, the change log of a catalog entry, and the **Updated by** filter.

<Info>
If your account has no name set, Studio opens **Complete your profile** instead and asks for it before you can carry on. That version of the dialog cannot be dismissed.
</Info>

## Change your password

Passwords are not managed in Studio. Sign out and use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page, or your identity provider if your organisation signs in through one.

## Sign out

Select **Sign out**. This ends the session for every environment you have access to, not just the one you are viewing.
