---
title: "Star a block as a favorite"
description: "Pin the sources, cards, segments and destinations you open most often to the Dashboard."
---

Favorites are per user and per environment. Starring a card in one environment does not star it in another, and your colleagues keep their own list.

## Star a block

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a listing">

    Go to **Sources**, **Cards**, **Segments** or **Destinations**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the star">

    Every tile carries a star in its lower-right corner. Click it to add the block to your favorites; click it again to remove it.

    ![The favourite star on a card tile](/images/guides/studio/dashboard/dashboard-favorite-star.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where they appear

Starred blocks show up under **Favorites** on the [Dashboard](/guides/studio/dashboard/overview), as the same tiles you see in the listings — status, last update, and the block's own counters.

![The Favorites strip on the dashboard](/images/guides/studio/dashboard/dashboard-favorites.png)

The strip scrolls sideways when you have more favorites than fit, and mixes block types in one row. Until you star something it shows an empty state instead.

<Info>
A destination tile carries its version selector. Favoriting a destination stars one specific API version, so a destination with two versions can appear twice.
</Info>
