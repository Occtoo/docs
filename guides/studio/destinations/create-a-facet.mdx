---
title: "Create a facet in the endpoint"
---

You also have the possibility to enable facet(s) for properties - a facet enables you to enrich your search query response. To enable facets for a property, click the **property name** in the endpoint and tick the
*"Enable facet"* tickbox on the subsequent dialogue.

![Enable facets](/images/guides/studio/destinations/enable-facets-eb69a8f3a787b0fd28e122f4cdade25a.png)

You have the possibility to add:

- **Facet Size** - Number of possible keys on the property - by default, this is set to 50.
- **Facet header** - Translation - to translate the header of the facet into different languages.

<Info>
If you add too many facets - there is a possibility that your endpoint might suffer performance wise.
</Info>

See example below:

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/destinations/destination-create-alias-3-19636b8aa9c01a4829cd2967506a13d7.png)
