---
title: "Create a facet in the endpoint"
---

You also have the possibillity to enable facet(s) for properties - a facet enables you to enrich your search query response. Ta enable facets for a property, click the **property name** in the enpoint and tick the
*"Enable facet"* tickbox on the subsequent dialogue.

![Enable facets](/images/guides/studio/destinations/enable-facets-eb69a8f3a787b0fd28e122f4cdade25a.png)

You have the possibilty to add:

- **Facet Size** - Amount of possible keys on the property - per default, this is set to 50.
- **Facet header** - Translation - to translate the header of the facet into different languages.

<Info>
If you add too many facets - there is a possibility that your endpoint might suffer performance wise.
</Info>
