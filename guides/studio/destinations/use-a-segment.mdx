---
title: "Use a segment in an endpoint"
---

If you opted to use a card when creating the endpoint - you also have the possibility to add a segment (if needed).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the endpoint">

    Open your endpoint in the detail view.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced settings">

    Click on **Advanced settings**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the card options">

    Click on the icon after the name of your card.

    ![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/destinations/destination-select-segment-1-3b2f10d590e8e21fa331e4afd9409788.png)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the segment">

    Select your Segment by clicking on either - or +.

    - **-** means that you exclude the segment selected.
    - **+** means that you include the segment selected.

    ![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/destinations/destination-select-segment-2-e87599882a57db8e2346bff586540b58.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can combine both included and excluded segments.
</Tip>

<Tip>
To be able to select a segment you need to have created a segment based on the card you are using.
</Tip>
