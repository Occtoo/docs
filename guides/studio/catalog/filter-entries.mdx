---
title: "Filter entries"
description: "Narrow the entry list with the filter panel, and turn the result into a query segment."
---

Search only matches entry titles. To narrow the list on any other property — a colour, a price range, a status, a date, a relation — open the **Filter** panel from the toolbar.

![The filter panel open over the Catalog page](/images/guides/studio/catalog/catalog-filter-panel.png)

The panel lists the card's filterable properties on the left with **Last updated (system)** pinned at the top, and the value editor for the selected property on the right. The footer counts how many entries match before you commit, and **Apply** puts the filters on the list.

<Info>
This is the same panel used when building a segment. [Filter segment entries](/guides/studio/segments/filter-segment-entries) documents every operator, the number, date, boolean and relation editors, the **Localized content** selector, and why some properties are greyed out.
</Info>

## What differs in Catalog

- Filters are **not saved**. They live on the page while you are on it, and switching card clears them.
- The **Filter** button carries a badge with the number of applied filters, so you can tell a filtered list from a full one.
- The **Localized content** selector only appears when the card's sources carry languages. On a card with no localized data the panel has no language control at all.

## Turn a filtered list into a segment

Once at least one filter is applied, **Create query segment** appears next to **Filter**.

![Create query segment next to a filtered result](/images/guides/studio/catalog/catalog-query-segment-focus.png)

<Steps>
<Step title="Filter the list">
Apply the filters that describe the entries you want. The entry count under the heading is the size of the segment you are about to create.
</Step>
<Step title="Press Create query segment">
Studio opens the segment creation form with the card and your filters already filled in, at the **Segment details** step.
</Step>
<Step title="Name the segment and save">
Fill in the name, description and tags. See [Create a segment](/guides/studio/segments/create-a-segment) for the rest of the form.
</Step>
</Steps>

A query segment keeps the criteria, not a list of ids — it re-evaluates as the data changes. To pin a fixed list of entries instead, select them in the list and see [Select entries](/guides/studio/catalog/select-entries).
