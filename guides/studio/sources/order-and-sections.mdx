---
title: "Display sections and order"
description: "Group source properties into sections and set the order they appear in."
---

Sections and order control how properties are laid out in the **Properties** widget and in the entry panel. They are presentation only — they change nothing about the data or what destinations receive.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Edit order and sections">

    In the **Properties** widget on the source page. The button is disabled when the source has no properties.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Rearrange">

    ![Reorganize display sections and their properties](/images/guides/studio/sources/order-and-sections.png)

    The full-screen dialog is titled **Reorganize display sections and their properties**, with the note "Drag properties to reorder them or move them between sections."

    - Drag a property by its handle to move it within a section or into another one.
    - Drag a section header to move the whole section.
    - **Collapse sections** folds every section; the button then reads **Expand sections**.
    - **Add new section** creates an empty section at the top.
    - The pencil on a section header renames it, and the remove icon on a section header deletes it.

    <Info>
    Deleting a section does not delete its properties — they move into the section above it, or the one below if it was first. A section cannot be deleted when it is the only one: "You cannot delete a section if it's the only one currently available".
    </Info>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Save">

    **Save** stays disabled until you move something. Occtoo confirms with "Order and sections updated".

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Cancel** discards every change made in the dialog. There is no per-property undo.
</Warning>

## The General section

**General** is where properties with no section assigned appear. It is not stored as a real section — leaving a property in **General** simply means it belongs to none. That is why **General** always exists, and why you cannot rename it away.

## Working with a filtered table

The reorder dialog always loads the source's full property list, even when the **Properties** widget behind it has a search filter applied. A save can never rewrite the order from a filtered subset.

<Info>
Reordering needs the data source edit permission. You can also set a single property's section from the property form — see [Add or edit a property](/guides/studio/sources/add-and-edit-a-property).
</Info>
