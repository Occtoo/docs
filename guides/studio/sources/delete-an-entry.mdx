---
title: "Delete an entry"
---

You have the possibility to Delete an entry if needed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the source">

    Open the source that you would like to delete an entry for

  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the entry">

    Serach for the entry you would like to delete

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on delete entry">

    Click on delete entry

    ![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/sources/delete-entry-51eadd2cbd86cd5a56cf5b71b7d3d9bf.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>
