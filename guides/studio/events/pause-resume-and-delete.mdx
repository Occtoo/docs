## Pause and resume

**Pause** sits at the top right of the subscription page and stops delivery immediately. The button becomes **Resume**, and the status becomes **Paused**.

<Warning>
**Resume** starts delivering from the current event head. Events published while the subscription was paused are never delivered — a pause drops them rather than queueing them.
</Warning>

If you need those events afterwards, read them from the [Events API](/api-reference/events/overview), which retains the stream independently of any subscription.

Neither button appears on a **Disabled** or **Deleted** subscription.

## Delete

Open the kebab menu and choose **Delete**.

![The delete confirmation](/images/guides/studio/events/delete-subscription.png)

Confirm with **Delete** and Studio returns to the listing. Deliveries stop immediately, and for a webhook the signing secret is revoked with it, so anything still holding that secret can no longer validate anything.

<Warning>
Deleting cannot be undone. Recreating the subscription means a new signing secret, a fresh endpoint verification, and delivery starting from the event head at that moment.
</Warning>

Pause instead when you only need the target to stop receiving for a while and want to keep the configuration.
