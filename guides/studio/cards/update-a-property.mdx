---
title: "Update an existing property"
---

- Open the card in a detailed view.
- Select the property you would like to update and press **Edit**.

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/cards/card-update-1-b8068af07d531e504eea6ac3f27e3f0c.png)

You can update the **Name** and **Description** of the property after the card has been published.

<Info>
If you need to change the identifier or the dataType of a property you will need to delete it first and create it again.
</Info>
