---
title: "Archive a card"
---

If a card is not in use anymore it can be archived, which means that the card can not be used and is no longer used in any destination.
Select the card and open it, select the menu in the upper right corner.

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/cards/card-archive-1-22ec0e8a32bb2f4d1a80eb259a8c91bc.png)

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/cards/card-archive-2-dbfcd1b78497ccac6b66d96079d95324.png)

<Warning>
The card will not be usable after you have archived it.
</Warning>

<Tip>
All archived cards can be found under the tab **Archived**.
</Tip>

![An image from the static](/images/guides/studio/cards/card-archive-3-c62251b84eff97a31b48b1e75ad06346.png)
