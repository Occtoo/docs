---
title: "Remove a property"
description: "Take a property off a card, and why the action is sometimes blocked."
---

Open the card, find the property in the **Properties** widget and press the minus icon on its row. Confirm in **Remove property <i>name</i>?** — "This property and all it's details will be removed from this card." — with **Yes, remove property**.

## When removal is blocked

The minus icon is disabled when the property is in use, with the tooltip "The property is in use and cannot be removed."

![Remove blocked because the property is in use](/images/guides/studio/cards/property-remove-blocked.png)

<Info>
  A property counts as in use when something downstream reads it — for example a
  segment filter or a destination. Remove it there first, then come back to the
  card.
</Info>

Removing also needs the card edit permission. Without it the icon is disabled and the tooltip says so.

## Draft cards versus active cards

- On a **Draft** card the property is taken off immediately.
- On an **Active** card the property stays in the table with status **Pending delete** and is only removed when you press **Activate card**.

While a removal is pending, the row offers a revert button instead of the edit pencil. Pressing it drops the pending removal and leaves the property in place. See [Work with pending changes](/guides/studio/cards/pending-changes).
