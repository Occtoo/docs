---
title: "Copy a card"
description: "Start a new card from an existing one, keeping its sources and property setup."
---

Copying a card is the quickest way to build a variation of a card that already works — the same sources and property setup, under a new name.

Open the card, open the menu in the upper right corner and pick **Copy card**.

<Info>
**Copy card** is available on **Active**, **Draft** and **Archived** cards. It is disabled while the card is **Updating** or **Archiving**: "Card is currently being updated and cannot be copied."
</Info>

Studio opens the [card creation flow](/guides/studio/cards/create-a-card), prefilled from the original:

- **Choose data source** holds the original's data sources.
- **Allow properties enrichment** carries over.
- The property selection step comes with the original's properties already ticked.
- **Name your card** and **Add a description** are empty.

![Copy card, first step](/images/guides/studio/cards/copy-card-step.png)

Give the copy a name and walk the remaining steps as usual, adjusting properties, display sections and display options along the way. Press **Save card** on the last step.

<Info>
The copy is created as a **Draft**, so it has no effect on any destination until you activate it. The original card is untouched.
</Info>

<Warning>
The name has to be unique. Cancelling the flow returns you to the card you copied from and keeps nothing.
</Warning>
