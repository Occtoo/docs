---
title: "Delete a card"
description: "Remove a card and all its details for good."
---

Open the card, open the menu in the upper right corner and pick **Delete card**. Confirm with **Yes, delete card**. Studio returns to the **Cards** page.

![Delete card dialog](/images/guides/studio/cards/delete-card-dialog.png)

<Warning>
Deleting is permanent: "This card and all its details will be deleted. This action cannot be undone." To take a card out of use but keep it, [archive it](/guides/studio/cards/archive-and-restore-a-card) instead.
</Warning>

## When deletion is blocked

The menu item is disabled when the card is in use, with the tooltip "Cards used in destinations, segments or relations cannot be deleted."

To delete such a card, first remove it from every destination and segment, and delete the relations it takes part in. The [usage widgets](/guides/studio/cards/track-usage) on the card page list everything you need to clear.

<Info>
Deletion is also blocked while the card is **Updating**: "Card is currently being updated and cannot be deleted." It needs the card delete permission — the same one as archiving.
</Info>

<Info>
A card's status does not by itself prevent deletion. An **Active** card with no segments, destinations or relations can be deleted; an unused **Archived** card can be deleted too.
</Info>
