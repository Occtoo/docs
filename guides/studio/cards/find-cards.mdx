The **Cards** page lists every card in the tenant. Each card shows its status, when it was last updated and how many properties it holds.

![Cards list page](/images/guides/studio/cards/cards-list.png)

## Sort and filter

Three controls sit above the list.

![Sort, creator and status filters on the Cards page](/images/guides/studio/cards/cards-list-filters-focus.png)

- **Sort by** — **Last updated (Newest first)** (the default), **Last updated (Oldest first)**, **Name ascending (A-Z)** or **Name descending (Z-A)**.
- **Created by** — narrows the list to cards created by one user.
- **Status** — see below.

Use **Search by card name** to match on name. Search, filters and sorting apply together.

## Status filter

The status list is split in two groups: **In use** and **Not in use**.

![Status filter open on the Cards page](/images/guides/studio/cards/cards-list-status-filter.png)

**All in use** is the default and shows everything except archived cards. To see archived cards, pick **Archived**.

<Info>
  Archived cards are hidden until you filter for them, so a card that seems to
  have disappeared from the list has usually been archived. See [Archive and
  restore a card](/guides/studio/cards/archive-and-restore-a-card).
</Info>

## Card statuses

- **Draft** — the card has never been activated. Properties can be added, changed and removed freely.
- **Active** — the card is in use and can be selected in segments and destinations.
- **Pending Changes** — the card is active and has uncommitted changes to its properties or relations. Activate the card to apply them.
- **Updating...** — changes are being applied. The card cannot be edited, archived, deleted or copied until this finishes.
- **Archiving...** — the card is being archived and should finish shortly.
- **Archived** — the card is not in use. It can be restored, copied or deleted.

<Info>
  The two in-progress statuses are shown with trailing dots on the card badge,
  while the **Status** filter lists them as **Updating** and **Archiving**.
</Info>

## Favourites

The star on each card pins it to your dashboard.
