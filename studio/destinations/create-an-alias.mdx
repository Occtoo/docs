---
title: "Create an alias in the endpoint"
---

In the endpoint you have the posibility to create aliases on your properties.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the endpoint">

    Click on your endpoint and it will open in a **Detail** view

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced settings">

    Click on Advanced settings

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the card">

    Click on the name of your card (that you would like to edit)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the alias">

    Click on the property you would like to create an alias for - create the alias

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">

    Click Save

    ![An image from the static](/images/destination-create-alias-1-deca54b3bb2fe920fbab01d216058fa9.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If your destination is Published you need to create a new version of the destination to be able to add aliases on the properties.
</Note>

![An image from the static](/images/destination-create-alias-2-b3464f00b5b7881b57982bb196ee9e8c.png)

If you have an alias created in a endpoint you will se an icon right to the name.
