---
title: "Reset a client secret"
---

If you lose your client secret you can reset the secret key.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the destination">

    Open your destination in the detail view.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Application tab">

    Click on the tab **Application**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the application menu">

    Click on the three dots (menu) icon under the **+ Add application**-button.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset the secret">

    Select **Reset secret**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the new secret key">

    You will receieve a new secret key.

    ![An image from the static](/images/destionation-reset-secret-a556ee135d2c17458ea9fbe022f469ab.png)

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
You are only able to see the secret key when creating the application - please write it down and save it.
If you loose it - you will have to reset it!
</Info>
