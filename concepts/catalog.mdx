---
title: "Catalog"
---

The catalog section is a powerful part of Occtoo, adding many new functionalities to the platform. It's a way for users to find and manage products, variants & SKUs with ease, as well as enriching them. The datamodel supports variants, hierarchies, localization, business logic and more to craft comprehensive product experiences for complex and configurable products.

![The Catalog page](/images/guides/studio/catalog/catalog-page.png)

Catalog shows the entries of one card at a time — the unified result of every source that feeds that card. Choose the card, search or filter your way to the entries you need, and open one to see and edit its values, its relations, its history and everything that publishes it.

<Info>
For how to work with it, see [Find entries](/guides/studio/catalog/find-entries), [Filter entries](/guides/studio/catalog/filter-entries) and [Enrich an entry](/guides/studio/catalog/enrich-an-entry).
</Info>
