---
title: "Catalog"
---

The catalog section is a powerful part of Occtoo, adding many new functionalities to the platform. It’s a way for users to find and manage products, variants & SKUs with ease, as well as enriching them. The datamodel supports variants, hierarchies, localization, business logic and more to craft comprehensive product experiences for complex and configurable products.

![An image from the static](/images/shared/catalogue-summary-1-b67299b62b0185a0d7e0cd8a7b521840.png)

The first view showcase all related data to a specific card. Choose the relevant card and search for your specific entries. To perform additional filtering to your current selection, press the ‘Filter’ button. A sidebar panel is then opened with the related data to your items. When the filters have been applied to your selection, the result will be displayed.

<Tip>
To find recently updated data, the sorting function can be used. When sorting by 'Last updated (Newest first)' all the latest updates will be displayed first.
</Tip>
