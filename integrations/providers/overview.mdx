---
title: "Providers"
---

A *provider* is an integration component that pushes data into Occtoo. It bridges a source system, application, or service and the Occtoo platform, transforming and mapping data from the source format into a format that Occtoo can process.

Depending on how the data is to be activated and used, a provider commonly splits and restructures the payloads it receives into several data sources.

## Ready-made integrations

We collaborate with selected software vendors to build standard connectors between Occtoo and their systems. These connectors are continuously monitored and updated to keep pace with changes in each respective system.

<Tip>
Are you a software vendor interested in a collaboration? Reach out to us at [developer@occtoo.com](mailto:developer@occtoo.com) and we will discuss how to get started.
</Tip>

### Bluestone PIM

The [pre-built integration for Bluestone PIM](/integrations/providers/bluestone-pim) exports products, categories, and media from [Bluestone PIM](https://www.bluestonepim.com/) into Occtoo.

### inRiver

The [generic inRiver provider](/integrations/providers/inriver) ships with extensions that are ready to deploy, so partners can send data from inRiver to Occtoo with minimal effort.

## Boilerplates

Boilerplates are pre-written, reusable templates that provide a foundation for developing your own provider. All of them are available through [Occtoo on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/Occtoo/) and are provided *"as is"* under the [MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit/).

<Tip>
Are you interested in sharing a boilerplate with the community? Reach out to us at [developer@occtoo.com](mailto:developer@occtoo.com) and we will discuss how to get started.
</Tip>
