---
title: "Remove an application"
---

If you have an application that is not in used anymore you can remove the application.
Upon removal - the access rights to the destination will be revoked.

- Open your destination in the detail view.
- Click on the tab **Application**.
- Click on the three dots (menu) icon under the **+ Add application**-button.
- Select **Remove the application**.
