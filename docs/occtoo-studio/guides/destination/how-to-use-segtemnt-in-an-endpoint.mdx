---
title: "Use a segment in a endpoint"
---

If you opted to use a card when creating the endpoint - you also have the possibility to add a segment (if needed).

- Open your endpoint in the detail view.
- Click on **Advanced settings**.
- Click on the icon after the name of your card.

![An image from the static](/images/destination-select-segment-1-3b2f10d590e8e21fa331e4afd9409788.png)

Select your Segment by clicking on either - or +.

- **-** means that you exclude the segment selected.
- **+** means that you include the segment selected.

![An image from the static](/images/destination-select-segment-2-e87599882a57db8e2346bff586540b58.png)

<Tip>
You can combined booth included and excluded segment.
</Tip>

<Tip>
To be able to select an assortment you need to have created an assortment based on the card you are using.
</Tip>
