## Partner perspective

This phase delivers the **highest ROI**—and the strongest long-term partner positioning.

Occtoo evolves into an **intelligent process and activation platform**, enabling customers to scale without scaling IT dependency.

<Note>
This is where partners move beyond delivery into operational and organizational impact.
</Note>

---

## What partners help configure

Using **Occtoo Studio**, partners help configure:

- User roles and access control
- Segment rules and queries
- Models and Data Cards
- Destinations
- Real-time APIs
- Product Data Logistics flows

This work is **configuration-driven**, not code-heavy.

---

## Enabling new roles

Occtoo allows responsibility to move closer to the business:

- Product managers
- Digital and marketing teams
- Sales and local market teams

<Tip>
Design governance models that balance autonomy with control—this is key to sustainable scale.
</Tip>
