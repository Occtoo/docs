## Partner perspective

**Data Activation** is where most Occtoo implementations begin—and where partners bring immediate, tangible value.

This phase focuses on connecting Occtoo to the customer’s surrounding data landscape and ensuring product data becomes **real-time, geo-scaled, and consumable** across all endpoints.

<Note>
This phase is often IT-led, but successful partners actively guide customers away from purely technical thinking toward long-term product experience enablement.
</Note>

---

## What partners typically deliver

Partners are responsible for:

- Integrating internal systems (ERP, PLM, CMS, Commerce)
- Connecting external sources (industry databases, marketplaces, syndication)
- Designing and implementing **Providers** and **Formatters**
- Onboarding data via Occtoo’s **model-agnostic ingestion API**

Occtoo supports onboarding virtually any data source while maintaining high performance and flexibility.

---

## Why this matters beyond integration

<Tip>
Position Data Activation as a **means to unlock product experiences**, not as an integration end goal.
</Tip>

Over-focusing on integration can:

- Lock customers into current-state solutions
- Reduce room for frontend and channel innovation
- Shift focus away from sales and business impact

Strong partners ensure that integration choices **enable**, rather than limit, future product orchestration.
